    <h1>Feelings &amp; emotional awareness</h1>
    <p>Help young people find words for what they feel. A simple creative prompt can make it easier to notice emotions and begin a supportive conversation.</p>
  </header>
  <section class="ocs__resource-topic-content">
    <h2>Start with curiosity, not a perfect answer.</h2>
    <p>Invite a child to choose colors, shapes, or images that match how a feeling shows up for them. There is no single correct way to make or interpret art; let them decide what they want to share.</p>
