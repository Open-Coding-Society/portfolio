    <h1>Caregiver &amp; family support</h1>
    <p>Caregivers can help children feel supported by making space for connection, patience, and small routines during big feelings or transitions.</p>
  </header>
  <section class="ocs__resource-topic-content">
    <h2>Connection can begin with one calm moment.</h2>
    <p>Try offering a quiet activity, naming what you notice without judgment, or giving a child time to respond. Choose an approach that feels comfortable and appropriate for your family.</p>
