    <h2 id="teen-support-title">If you want to talk to someone</h2>
    <p>Reaching out can feel awkward, but you deserve support. Consider a trusted adult, school counselor, health professional, or one of these services in the United States:</p>
    <div class="ocs__resource-topic-links">
      <a href="https://988lifeline.org/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><strong>988 Suicide &amp; Crisis Lifeline</strong><span>Call or text 988 for free, confidential support, 24/7.</span></a>
      <a href="https://www.crisistextline.org/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><strong>Crisis Text Line</strong><span>Text HOME to 741741 to connect with a trained crisis counselor.</span></a>
    </div>
    <p class="ocs__resource-topic-note">If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency department. These links do not replace professional care.</p>
